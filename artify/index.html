✦   About Artify

Artify is a browser-based photo editor that uses image-processing techniques to turn your photos into pencil sketches or cartoon-like images. It applies grayscale, blur, color-dodge shading, color quantization, and Sobel edge detection.

Your images are processed locally. No login, server, or installation is required.
